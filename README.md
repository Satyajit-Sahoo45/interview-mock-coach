**Mock Interview Coach** is an AI-powered interview preparation platform that helps job seekers practice and improve their interview skills through realistic mock interviews. Users can customize interview sessions based on role, interview type, and difficulty level, then receive instant AI-generated feedback on their responses.

The platform simulates real interview experiences by asking relevant technical and behavioral questions, evaluating answers, generating follow-up questions, and providing detailed performance insights. Users can track their interview history, review past sessions, identify strengths and weaknesses, and monitor improvement over time.

Built with React, Vite, Clerk Authentication, Supabase, and Google's Gemini AI, the application offers a modern, secure, and responsive experience with cloud-based session storage, personalized settings, and intelligent feedback tailored to each user's career goals.

**Key Features:**

* AI-generated mock interviews
* Technical and behavioral interview modes
* Personalized feedback and scoring
* Follow-up question generation
* Interview history and analytics
* Secure authentication with Clerk
* Cloud storage with Supabase
* Responsive and user-friendly interface

Mock Interview Coach empowers candidates to build confidence, refine communication skills, and prepare effectively for real-world job interviews.
